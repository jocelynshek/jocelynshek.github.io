I'm a senior at Harvard University studying sociology and statistics. Basically, I'm passionate about understanding people and the systems that shape everyday life through data! A lifelong storyteller, I came to college planning to study theater (though am still heavily involved!), but fell in love with uncovering human experiences through a new medium: journalism. With a background in quantitative social science research, I care about making rigorous social analysis legible to wider audiences through rich reporting and engaging visuals.
